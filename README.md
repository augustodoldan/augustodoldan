## Español

Hola, soy Augusto.

Soy Analista de Sistemas (ORT) y programador autodidacta, con un background técnico fuerte. Hoy soy Product Manager en una plataforma de logística de última milla, y entiendo el mundo IT desde adentro: hablo el mismo idioma que el equipo de desarrollo, así que decidimos mejor qué construir y cómo, sin prometer lo que no se puede.

Siempre fui programador, pero terminaba haciendo producto: era el programador al que le gustaba tener reuniones. Por eso fui migrando de a poco hasta llegar a este rol.

Pasé por startups, logística, fintech y en uno de los bancos más importantes de Argentina. En cada lugar aprendí algo distinto, y de todos me quedó lo mismo: cada problema pide su propia herramienta.

Por eso no me caso con una tecnología. Laburé en web y en mobile, y también con apps multiplataforma, con Flutter y Capacitor, además de Java, JavaScript y TypeScript. Sé cuándo conviene ir multiplataforma, cuándo alcanza con React, Vite y un Express, cuándo hace falta un backend en Java o Python y cuándo vale la pena ir directo con Next.

## English

Hi, I'm Augusto.

I'm a Systems Analyst (ORT) and a self-taught programmer with a strong technical background. Today I'm a Product Manager on a last-mile logistics platform, and I understand the IT world from the inside: I speak the same language as the dev team, so we make better decisions about what to build and how, without promising what can't be done.

I've always been a developer, but I kept ending up doing product: I was the programmer who actually enjoyed meetings. So I gradually moved over until I reached this role.

I've worked in startups, logistics, fintech and at one of the largest banks in Argentina. Each place taught me something different, and they all left me with the same lesson: every problem asks for its own tool.

So I'm not married to any one technology. I've worked on web and mobile, and also on cross-platform apps, with Flutter and Capacitor, plus Java, JavaScript and TypeScript. I know when going cross-platform makes sense, when React, Vite and an Express server are enough, when a backend in Java or Python is needed and when it's worth going straight to Next.

> *"Fall in love with the problem, not the solution."*
> — Uri Levine, co-founder of Waze

It's the line that best sums up how I work: understand the problem deeply first, and only then think about the solution, which rarely comes out right the first time.

I'm also a lawyer, and it shows in two ways. First, I have strong negotiation skills and I'm comfortable working with C-levels: I sit down with leadership, listen to what each side needs and land on an agreement that can actually be executed. Second, I like to be very clear about what we're going to do before we start, because what isn't written down doesn't exist, in a contract or in a ticket.

If you'd like to chat, message me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/augustodoldan/). Always happy to hear from you.
